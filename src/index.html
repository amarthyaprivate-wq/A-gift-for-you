To Blessy
The girl I met just this Monday

It’s hardly been a week since I met you, yet somehow, I’ve already started regretting the 22 years I lived without knowing you existed.

I honestly wish I had met you the day I was born, so I could have known you for all 22 years instead of just a few days.

We may be hundreds, even thousands of kilometres apart, but somehow, you’ve become the closest anyone has ever been to my heart. You came into my life so suddenly, yet you managed to reach a place in my heart that no one else ever could. You washed away the stains that life had left behind and brought a kind of happiness I never knew I was missing.

I envy Shakespeare and every poet who ever wrote about love, because even they seem to have had better words than I do. No matter how hard I try, I can’t find words beautiful enough to describe what you make me feel.

So I’ll simply say this—

Thank you for coming into my life, Blessy.
You are my most precious and one-and-only diamond. Yours always
